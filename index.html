Terms of Service

1. What the tool is

Grandaya Publicador is a private desktop tool that runs on the account owner's own computer. It takes short vertical videos produced for the Grandaya brand, adds background music, and uploads them to the Grandaya accounts on TikTok (@grandaya6), YouTube (@grandaya) and Instagram (@grandaya_) on a schedule of up to three videos per day.

2. Who may use it

The tool is operated only by the owner of the Grandaya accounts. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and only connects accounts that the operator owns and has authorized through each platform's official login.

3. Content responsibility

The operator is responsible for every video the tool uploads, including holding the rights to the footage, voice and music, and for following the community guidelines and terms of TikTok, YouTube and Instagram.

4. Platform terms

Use of each connected platform remains subject to that platform's own terms. Access can be revoked at any time from the platform's settings (in TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions).

5. No warranty

The tool is provided as is. Uploads can fail or be delayed because of network problems, platform limits or changes to the platforms' APIs.

6. Changes

These terms may be updated. The effective date at the top of this page shows the current version.

Privacy Policy

Data the tool accesses

  • Basic profile information of the connected account (display name, avatar, open ID) through the user.info.basic scope, used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos to that account (video.upload, video.publish).
  • The access and refresh tokens issued by each platform.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the operator's own videos to the operator's own accounts. The tool does not read followers, messages, comments, analytics or any data about other users.

Storage

Tokens are stored in local files on the operator's computer, readable only by the operator's user account. The tool has no server and no database, and sends nothing to anyone other than the platforms' official APIs.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the account stays connected. Revoking access in the platform's settings, or deleting the local credential files, removes the tool's access immediately.
